How to get a FREE Google Gemini API Key (No credit card required)
  1. Go to Google AI Studio (aistudio.google.com/apikey) and sign in with your regular Google account.
  2. Click the blue “Create API key” button (accept the terms of service if it’s your first visit, and choose a project if prompted).
  3. Click Copy next to your new key (it starts with AIza...).
  4. Paste the key into the Gemini API Key field above — FeastSync will automatically fetch your available models and select a fast, free-tier Lite model!
